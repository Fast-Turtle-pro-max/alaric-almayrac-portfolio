Complex Analysis

Complex analysis, traditionally known as the theory of functions of a complex variable, is the branch of mathematical analysis that investigates functions of complex numbers.
I had the chance of following a class on this topic during my third year at university. It immediately became one of my favourite topics I ever studied.
It was a real fresh take on analysis and a new way of seeing things, and with the introduction of holomorphic functions a new world opened to us.

Object-oriented programming

Object-oriented programming (OOP) is a programming paradigm based on the object – a software entity that encapsulates data and function(s).
A course I followed during my first year of university in Avignon.
It was a really interesting topic: it showed new horizons and the basis of programming used in today's world, in particular in video games or graphics engines to make the CGI used in the film industry.
